The Master Builder

Act 3

Scene: The large broad verandah of Solness's dwelling-house

HILDA

[Rises with vehemence, and makes a gesture of repulsion with her hand.]

Yes, to be sure it is! Castles in the air—they are so easy to build, too—especially for the builders who have a—a dizzy conscience.

SOLNESS

You say I do.

HILDA

Well—you admit you owe me this kingdom. And you can't have a kingdom without a royal castle, I should think.
